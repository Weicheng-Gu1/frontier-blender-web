Reading the results

Scores are out of 100; higher is better. Overall is the mean across the 80 tasks. Category columns average tasks within each category. Only complete configurations in the approved paper selection are shown.

Average tokens include prompt and completion tokens, including cached input, without double-counting reasoning. Average cost is the per-task USD cost from recorded CLI charges or published API-rate calculations. These figures describe the dated release, not ongoing jobs.
